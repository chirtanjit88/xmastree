A LITTLE CARE GOES A LONG WAY.

A real Christmas tree brings the fragrance and beauty of the outdoors into your home. With a little water and the right care, you can help your tree stay looking its best throughout the festive season.

START FRESH. KEEP
IT BEAUTIFUL

The best tree care begins as soon as your tree is cut. Follow these simple steps to help it absorb water and settle into its new home.

  • 1
    Make a fresh cut

    Cut 20mm off the bottom of the stump – This opens up the tree up to water.

  • 2
    Get it into water quickly

    Put the stump of your tree into water as soon as possible – Check water supply daily.

  • 3
    Keep it cool.

    If you do not wish to put your tree into water immediately store in a cool place away from the sun and Wind.

THREE SIMPLE STEPS
TO A HAPPIER TREE

Check the stand's water reservoir every day, especially during the first few days

Position your tree away from fireplaces, heaters, and direct heat sources.

Look at the water level, branches, and needles each day.

Note: When first put into water a Christmas tree can drink 1 to 4 liters of water in the first day!

A STRONG STAND MAKES
ALL THE DIFFERENCE.

A suitable stand keeps your tree upright and provides a reservoir for water. Choose one that fits your tree's trunk and height, and make sure it remains stable. Keep the reservoir accessible so you can check and refill it every day.

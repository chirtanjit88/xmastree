VISITOR FAQS

When you come to pick your Christmas Tree this year why not take the opportunity to take your own photo in front of The Largest Christmas Tree in Australia! Bookings are not strictly required for standard visits, but we recommend booking early for weekend visits and special events.

Yes, absolutely! We are pet-friendly. Friendly dogs kept on leashes are always warmly welcome to join the family holiday tradition.

We recommend comfortable closed-toe walking shoes or boots suitable for farm trails, as well as a hat and sunscreen on sunny Victorian days.

We accept EFTPOS, major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard), as well as cash across all our farm payment counters and Ma's Christmas Corner shop.

Most families enjoy spending between 45 minutes to 2 hours wandering the tree rows, selecting their ideal tree, enjoying photos, and shopping for decorations at Ma's Christmas Corner.
